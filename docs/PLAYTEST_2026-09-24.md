## Still open

- **Camera zoom-in when an enemy steps between lens and player (5:57.0 → 5:57.2).** Popper treats
  any opaque, colliding part as an occluder, and the Humanoid forces CanCollide on an enemy's torso
  and head every step, so simply turning the torso's collision off does not stick. Needs a play check
  first, then either a collision-group approach or the camera module's occlusion filter.
