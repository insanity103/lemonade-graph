# Frostbound Glacier gauntlet: live progress

The bar: the three photos in `docs/map/glacier_refs/`. Ours must be more striking, and still read
as a toy. Each piece has a builder and a separate critic; the critic sees ours and the photo
unlabelled and picks one.

| Piece | Photo | Round | Critic's pick | Biggest remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ice walls and the range | glacier_valley | 1 | photo | No height hierarchy and no skyline: same-height columns read as a crate of crystal sticks. Wants 3-5 huge peaks 3-4x taller, near columns cut down to rubble, a far ring of pale mountains with sky between, deep-blue shaded faces with navy fissures, and a frozen river leading to a notch. |
| Crevasse canyon and crystals | ice_canyon | 1 | photo | Reads as a blue hallway: flat slabs in one mid-blue, no sky, no height. Wants walls 2-3x taller with an open sky slot, shading from white rim to navy floor, staggered ledges in three ice colours with snow caps, and deliberate crystal landmarks instead of a tumble. |
| Frost Hollow village | snow_village | 1 | photo | Lit at noon with no contrast: windows flat white, snow/ice/sky in one pale range. Wants dusk-blue ambient, warm glowing windows and amber pools of light on blue snow. |

- Round 2, village: builder launched (dusk-blue air, amber windows, smoke, second row of roofs, pond and fire in the foreground; also the render script's lighting).
- Round 1, canyon: builder made layered leaning strata walls, geodes, an ice-block floor and a sunset cleft. Critic picked the photo. Gap: no height or tonal range. Round 2 builder launched.
