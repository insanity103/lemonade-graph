## Game Mechanics (Inferred)

> **Note:** No official wiki or documentation exists. The following mechanics are inferred from gamepass descriptions and the game's own description.

### Core Loop
1. **Explore** areas/regions in the game world
2. **Fight enemies** to gain XP and Gold
3. **Collect weapon drops** (random drop system with65+ unique weapons)
4. **Level up** to access new areas and content
5. **Rebirth** to reset progress for permanent bonuses
6. **PVP** other players using collected weapons

### Weapon Drop System
- Swords drop randomly from enemies
- Drop rates are probabilistic (RNG-based)
- A gamepass exists to increase drop rates by25%
- The goal is to collect all65+ weapons
- Different weapons presumably have different rarities/stats (not documented)

### Rebirth System
- Players can "rebirth" (prestige/reset)
- Rebirth resets level progress but provides permanent bonuses
- Double XP/Gold gamepass persists through rebirths
- Dual Weaponary gamepass persists through rebirths
- Rebirth likely increases base stats or unlocks content

### Health Regeneration
- Health regens passively over time
- Regen rate can be modified by equipment and gamepasses:
  - Legendary Pack:2.5x health regen (described as "OP")
  - Devil Armor:25% faster health regen
  - Effects can stack

### Walkspeed
- Base walkspeed exists as a stat
- Can be modified by equipment:
  - Dark Dragon Armor (from Legendary Pack): nearly double normal walkspeed
  - Devil Armor: high walkspeed boost
- Fire torso visual effect comes with armor gamepasses (cosmetic, may obstruct first-person view)
