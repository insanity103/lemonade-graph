### 2.2 Roblox Official Recommendations for Developer Products

Per Roblox's official documentation, developer products are ideal for:
- **In-game currency** (sold in tiers with volume discounts)
- **Consumable items** (potions, ammo, temporary boosts)
- **One-time-use services** (stat resets, name changes)

**Best Practice:** Use `ProcessReceipt` API for secure handling. Offer tiered packages with **better value at higher price points** (anchoring effect).

### 7.4 The "Pay to Progress" vs "Pay to Win" Line

**What players consider ACCEPTABLE ("Pay to Progress"):**
- 2x currency/XP boosts (speeds up, doesn't add power)
- Fast boats/vehicles (convenience, not combat advantage)
- Stat resets (available free elsewhere)
- Fruit storage expansion (utility)
- Private servers (peaceful play)
- Cosmetics (no stat impact)

**What players consider UNACCEPTABLE ("Pay to Win"):**
- Exclusive weapons only available for Robux (Dark Blade in Blox Fruits)
- Abilities locked behind Robux (Conquerors Haki in King Legacy)
- Stat advantages that cannot be earned in-game
- Gacha mechanics that require real money for competitive builds (Shindo Life)
- PvP-affecting items sold for Robux

**The Golden Rule:** If a F2P player can eventually achieve the same power level through gameplay (even if slower), the community generally accepts it. If content is **permanently locked** behind a paywall, it's considered P2W.

## 9. Monetization Best Practices

### 9.1 Principles from Top Roblox Sword RPGs

#### ✅ DO: Sell Convenience, Not Power
- 2x boosts are the **#1 most recommended** purchase across ALL games
- Players feel good buying them because they enhance gameplay, not replace it
- Always provide a free (slower) path to the same outcome

#### ✅ DO: Create a "First Purchase" Pipeline
The most successful on-ramp pattern across top games:
1. **Free codes** → Give new players a taste of boosted progress
2. **First purchase suggestion** → After codes expire, show 2x Money/XP pass
3. **Private server** → Once invested, suggest private server for peaceful grinding
4. **Storage/utility** → As players accumulate items, suggest expansion

#### ✅ DO: Use Tiered Pricing with Volume Discounts
Blox Fruits currency packages demonstrate this perfectly:
- 50 Robux → 10,000 Money (200 Money/R$)
- 1,499 Robux → 500,000 Money (333 Money/R$)
- Higher tiers give **~65% more value**, encouraging bigger purchases

#### ✅ DO: Offer Limited-Time Bundles
- Create urgency with 1-3 week availability windows
- Bundle 3-5 items at 15-25% discount vs individual purchase
- Include one exclusive item (mutation, skin) to drive FOMO

#### ✅ DO: Allow Trading of Purchased Items
- Blox Fruits' trading system for gamepasses is brilliant
- F2P players can obtain premium items by trading in-game valuables
- This creates a player-driven economy that extends engagement
- Purchased items gain perceived value beyond their Robux cost

#### ✅ DO: Make Private Servers Available
- Consistently one of the highest-rated purchases
- Solves a real player pain point (griefing, competition for spawns)
- Creates recurring social value (play with friends)

#### ❌ DON'T: Sell Exclusive Combat-Advantage Items
- Dark Blade (Blox Fruits) and Conquerors Haki (King Legacy) are the most criticized passes
- Even if the weapon is only "good" and not "best," exclusive pay-only weapons create resentment

#### ❌ DON'T: Use Gacha/Lootbox for Core Progression
- Shindo Life's spin system is heavily criticized
- If using gacha, ensure **all items are obtainable free** with gacha being purely a shortcut
- Roblox has specific [Randomized Virtual Item Policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items) rules

#### ❌ DON'T: Price Entry-Level Passes Above 500 Robux
- The psychological "impulse buy" threshold on Roblox is ~500 Robux (~$5.50)
- 2x Money at 450 Robux hits this sweet spot perfectly
- Higher-priced items should offer proportionally more value

### 9.2 Pricing Psychology Observed

| Technique | Example | Effectiveness |
|---|---|---|
| **Anchoring** | Ultimate Bundle (9,999 R$) makes 3,999 R$ bundles look cheap | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Volume Discount** | 24h EXP boost costs 60x the 15min boost but gives 96x the time | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **First Purchase Hook** | 50 Robux currency packs lower the barrier to first payment | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **FOMO** | Limited-time bundles with 10-day windows | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Social Proof** | "Most Popular" tags on 2x Money pass | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Loss Aversion** | "You'll lose your EXP boost when it expires!" | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Sunk Cost** | After buying 1 pass, players are more likely to buy more | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Trading Value** | Fruit Storage has high trade value, creating social currency | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

### 9.3 How Top Games Introduce New Players to the Shop

**Blox Fruits Pattern:**
1. New player joins → Gets free EXP code from social media
2. Uses code → Experiences 2x EXP for 20 minutes
3. Code expires → "2x EXP Boost" in shop suddenly looks appealing
4. Player grinds to Sea 2 → Discovers Stat Reset NPC (free)
5. If player wants reset in Sea 1 → Stat Reset dev product (75 R$) is available
6. Player reaches trading → Realizes Fruit Storage has trade value
7. Player engages with PvP → Considers 2x Boss Drops for better gear

**King Legacy Pattern:**
1. New player joins → Notices fruit spawning on map
2. Can't find fruit → Fruit Position pass (2,500 R$) becomes tempting
3. Player grinds quests → Notices money is slow → Quest Beli x2 (1,000 R$)
4. Player discovers Legacy Island → Needs Legacy Pose (350 R$) or Spawn pass (275 R$)

- Season quests: 10 quests × 500 BP XP = 5,000 BP XP/season
- Tier cost: 500 BP XP per tier
- **F2P completion:** ~4 weeks of regular play (achievable)
- **Premium skip path:** Immediate 15 tiers + normal progression

### 10.4 Ethical Monetization Guidelines

#### The Sword RPG Fair Monetization Pledge

1. **No Exclusive Power:** Every weapon, ability, and stat boost available for free through gameplay
2. **Time vs Money:** Paying speeds up progression; it never gates it
3. **Transparent Odds:** If any random/reward system exists, display exact percentages
4. **No Predatory FOMO:** Items return on rotation; "limited" items come back annually
5. **Respect Young Players:** No dark patterns, no guilt mechanics, no "energy" systems
6. **Free Alternatives:** Every developer product should have an in-game NPC/code equivalent
7. **Trading Economy:** Let F2P players trade for premium items using in-game effort
8. **PvP Balance:** PvP-relevant items must be earnable free; cosmetic-only premium
9. **Clear Value:** Show exact quantities and probabilities before purchase
10. **Generous Codes:** Regular promotional codes for boosts/currency to maintain goodwill

#### Monetization Health Check Questions

Before releasing any monetized item, answer:
- [ ] Can a F2P player eventually obtain this or its equivalent?
- [ ] Does this item give an advantage in PvP that cannot be earned otherwise?
- [ ] Would we be comfortable if a content creator reviewed this item publicly?
- [ ] Is the price consistent with similar items in competing games?
- [ ] Does this item enhance fun or just remove friction?
- [ ] Are odds/rates displayed clearly if randomness is involved?

## Appendix C: Key Takeaways for Sword RPG Design

### The "Big Three" Monetization Pillars

1. **Speed (2x Boosts)** — The bread and butter. Highest volume, fairest perception. Price at 399-499 R$.
2. **Convenience (Private Servers, Fast Travel, Detectors)** — Quality of life. Moderate price, high satisfaction. Price at 299-599 R$.
3. **Collection (Permanent Items, Storage, Cosmetics)** — Whale territory. Highest price ceiling, longest engagement. Price at 199-5,000 R$.

### Revenue Model Insight

Based on analysis of Blox Fruits (the top-grossing Roblox game):
- **70%+ of revenue** likely comes from permanent fruit purchases (50-5,000 R$ each)
- **2x boosts** are the **gateway purchase** — most players' first buy
- **Bundles** drive **spike revenue** during events/holidays
- **Developer products** (currency packs) provide **steady recurring revenue**
- **Trading system** converts F2P engagement into premium currency circulation

### The F2P Sweet Spot

The most successful Roblox sword RPGs follow this formula:
> **"Everything free, something faster for money, nothing exclusive for power."**

Games that violate this (Shindo Life's gacha, King Legacy's Conquerors Haki) face consistent community backlash, while games that follow it (GPO's cosmetic model, Blox Fruits' tradeable passes) maintain goodwill even with high spending ceilings.
